# Legal

UOTerm is for people who run their own shards, and for researchers.

- Ultima Online is a trademark of its owners. UOTerm is independent. It has no tie to them.
- This repository does not ship game files (MUL, UOP, idx, art, sound or map data). Do not share them. When you need the map, the art or the walk data, use a client folder you got legally.
- Do not sell or promote UOTerm as a bot for official servers. Do not use it to cheat, to hide from detection, or to farm on EA or Broadsword shards.
- Use it on private shards, demo servers and offline worlds that the operator controls.
- The Terms of Service of an official server may forbid other clients. The operator and each user must follow the shard rules and the Terms of Service that apply.
- If the shard rules say AI players must be named as such, the operator must tell the other players.
- `--encryption none|osi` picks how the client talks on the wire. `osi` is the same kind of stream cipher a Classic Client uses. It is not a way around the security of official servers, and it does not hide the client. This repository does not ship cracked keys.
