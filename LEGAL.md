# Legal

UOTerm is for operators of shards they control and for researchers.

- Ultima Online is a trademark of its owners. UOTerm is independent and unaffiliated.
- Do not distribute game assets (MUL, UOP, idx, art, sound, map data). Users supply a legitimate client directory when map walkability is required. This repository does not ship MUL or UOP files.
- Do not market UOTerm as an official-server bot or farm bot. Do not use it to cheat, evade detection, or farm EA/Broadsword shards.
- Target private shards, demo servers, and offline worlds the operator controls.
- Official Terms of Service may forbid third-party clients. Operators and users must follow shard rules and the applicable Terms of Service.
- If the shard's rules require disclosure of AI players, the operator must disclose them to other players.
- Encryption (`--encryption none|osi`) is a client codec, the same class of stream cipher a Classic Client uses. It is not a cracked-key bypass of official servers. It is not an anti-detect feature. This repository does not ship cracked keys.
