# Legal

UOTerm is for operators of shards they control and for researchers.

- Ultima Online is a trademark of its owners. UOTerm is independent and unaffiliated.
- This repository does not ship game assets (MUL, UOP, idx, art, sound, map data), and you must not distribute them. Users supply a legitimate client directory when they need the map, the art, or walkability from map files.
- Do not market UOTerm as an official-server bot or farm bot. Do not use it to cheat, evade detection, or farm EA or Broadsword shards.
- Target private shards, demo servers, and offline worlds the operator controls.
- Official Terms of Service may forbid third-party clients. Operators and users must follow the shard rules and the applicable Terms of Service.
- If the shard rules require disclosure of AI players, the operator must disclose them to other players.
- Encryption (`--encryption none|osi`) is a client codec, the same class of stream cipher a Classic Client uses. It is not a cracked-key bypass of official servers and not an anti-detect feature. This repository does not ship cracked keys.
