# Containers playbook

1. `use` the container (corpse, chest, pack, bank box).
2. Read `observe.containers`. `total` is how many items it really holds. The
   list is cut short.
3. `lift` an item, then `drop` it into the pack or onto the ground.
4. A bag inside a bag: `use` the inner bag where it is, then lift from that
   serial.
5. If a lift is refused (`lift_rejected`), the item is gone, too heavy, or
   out of reach.

The bank box is the container you wear on the bank layer, after you say
`bank`.
