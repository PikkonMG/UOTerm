# Inspect playbook

1. `observe` gives the radar and the mobiles near you. `size` (5 to 41,
   default 21) makes the radar wider. The CLI `uoterm look` is the same
   picture at size 21.
2. `single_click` a mobile or an item to ask its name. Wait for the name in
   the journal.
3. `look_around` describes a wider scene. `find_mobiles` and `find_items`
   search.
4. `map_tile` and `can_walk` check a tile before you step onto it.

Names are often empty at first sight. Click before you hunt or buy by name.
