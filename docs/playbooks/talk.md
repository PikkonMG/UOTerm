# Talk playbook

The client tells you when someone spoke to this character (`spoken_to`, `unanswered`). You write the words.

1. On `speech` or `spoken_to`, read `observe`: doing, nearby people, last journal.
2. `reply` answers the last unanswered line. `say` / `whisper` when you start the talk.
3. Match what the character is already doing. If she follows, do not say she will stay.
4. `play_along` false: refuse plans except friends. True: party, follow, fight as the persona lists, with a time limit.
5. Near bankers and vendors, do not say `bank`, `buy`, `sell`, `guards`, or the vendor's name unless you mean the command.

Do not answer your own speech. Party and guild lines count as spoken_to even out of sight.
