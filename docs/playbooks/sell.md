# Sell playbook

Selling is not buying. The sell list is items in your pack, and it often stays open after a sale.

1. Stand next to the vendor. Say `<Name> sell`.
2. `vendor_sell` with graphic or serial and amount.
3. Confirm gold. The list may still be open; you can sell again without reopening.
4. Do not use a remembered buy-list serial. Those are vendor stock, not your items.
