# Mounts playbook

`use` on a mobile while war mode is on is an attack. The `mount` and
`dismount` tools turn war mode off first and then double-click, so use
them. In a fight, both are refused. By hand: turn war mode off, wait until
`observe` shows `war` false, then `use` the animal.

1. Your animal: a mobile the shard marks as yours, or the mount layer while
   you ride.
2. Mount: war mode off, cancel an old target, `use` the animal. Check the
   mount layer, or that the animal is gone as a mobile.
3. Dismount: `use` **your own serial**, not the animal. A `use` on the
   animal again does not dismount.
4. After you dismount, say `all follow me` so the pet walks with you.

Do not hunt while you still need the mount near you, unless you accept that
it may get hit.
