# Buy playbook

1. Find the vendor (`find_mobiles`, then `single_click` for the real name).
2. Stand next to them. Say `<Name> buy` (the speech keyword form). Plain words without the keyword packet do not open the shop on many shards. `say` in UOTerm already encodes keywords.
3. `vendor_buy` with graphic or name and amount.
4. A buy often closes the list. Open it again before a second buy. Serials on the list are new each time; look up by name, not a remembered serial.
5. Confirm gold and pack.

`set_goal` `shop` walks to a nearby innocent or the bank when you have no vendor in mind.
